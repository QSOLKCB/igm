## Research software only

IGM produces computational models and observations. A model may be mathematically exact and computationally reproducible while remaining biologically wrong.

**Perfect Mathematics Does Not Equal Perfect Biological Reality.**

Biological interpretation and validation require evidence and expertise outside the runtime.
